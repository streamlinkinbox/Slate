The three sets

One geometry definition per icon, three paint recipes. Roles stay put, so switching sets never reflows the artwork.

Monotone

Flat currentColor, uniform weight, no fills. For toolbars, ribbons and dense UI at 16–20px.

Duotone

Primary line work at full tone, construction geometry at 38%, silhouette tinted at 16%. Reads depth without colour.

Colour

Semantic hues by tool family — draw, modify, transform — over graphite line work. For empty states and onboarding.

Geometry

Rules that keep the set optically consistent when icons sit side by side.

Grid & safe area

24×24 viewBox, 18×18 live area, 3px padding. Terminals may bleed to 1.5px from the edge so round caps optically align.

Weight

1.5px stroke everywhere — primary, secondary, accent and node dots alike. Node dots are 3px round caps, never scaled circles.

Terminals

Round caps and joins throughout. Dashed construction lines switch to butt caps so the dash rhythm stays even.

Roles

primary subject · secondary guides & removed material · accent focal detail · soft silhouette fill.

Palette

Colour set only. Monotone and duotone inherit currentColor and need no tokens.
